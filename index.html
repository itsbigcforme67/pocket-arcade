Bring your own files

No BIOS is included, and the only games are our own: Tricky Wicks and the minigames. Each console asks for your own dumps the first time.

Nothing is uploaded

Your files, saves and progress stay in this browser's storage on this device. QuickFlip only shares a note when you upload it to a server you set up.

Works like an app

Most consoles here can be installed with your browser's “Add to Home Screen” and then played offline.
