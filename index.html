Bring your own files

No BIOS and no games are included. Each console asks for your own dumps the first time.

Nothing is uploaded

Your files, saves and progress stay in this browser's storage on this device.

Works like an app

Use your browser's “Add to Home Screen” inside a console to install it and play offline.
