VMX

The VMX is a pocket handheld shaped like an original Xbox Memory Unit: a 64 × 64 one-bit picture on a green OLED, a D-pad, A and B, and a beeper. Press any button to switch it on.

Menus: A opens, B goes back. Games: left/right picks, A plays, Down shows a demo. In a game, the side button (the strip on the right edge) goes back to the games; so does B in games that don't use it. Hold the side button to switch off and on. Left alone in the menus, the games play themselves.

↑↓←→ D-pad   Z/Space A   X B   Esc side button. Pads work too.
