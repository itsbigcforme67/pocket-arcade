Games in this folder come with the site: they are published, and the gb/ page lists them under
"Free game" with a download link. The list itself is the "free" block in gb/index.html.

Only put a game here if it is yours to give away, or its licence allows it. Anything else belongs
in roms/gb/, which is never published.
